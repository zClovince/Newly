Privacy

All local. Settings stay in your browser's own storage, the video and icons in an offline cache. No accounts, no tracking, no GPS. Your weather city goes to Open-Meteo, and — only while Qur'an verses are on — that same city goes to Aladhan for prayer times. That's all.
